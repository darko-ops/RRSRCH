Superseded. Failed payments were once retried every 12 hours for up to 8 attempts, with no defined grace period. The cadence annoyed customers and the lack of a grace window cut off access too aggressively. It was replaced by the 1/3/5/7-day dunning schedule with a 7-day grace period. Kept for history; do not build on this.
