WHAT CHANGED IN PHASE 2 (all deterministic, keyless):
  · supersession  — a replaced item (declared via `supersedes`) is Archive-only,
                    like stale. Belt-and-suspenders correctness.
  · graph boost   — an item `related` to a strong match is lifted, so a
                    load-bearing fact that is lexically weak but topically
                    adjacent can still earn a place (recall).
  · compression   — a two-pass packer: fill in the smallest faithful form
                    (authored `brief`, else sentence-trim) to MAXIMISE coverage,
                    then upgrade back to full text with leftover budget for detail.

NO REGRESSION: the original 10 cases (budget 2500) are byte-identical to
baseline.txt. Compression/graph-boost only activate when the budget actually
bites — at 2500 there is always room to upgrade every item back to full text, so
the pack equals the Phase-1 pack. The new headroom case (06) is where the budget
bites and the Phase-2 machinery is visible.
