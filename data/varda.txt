Varda Space Industries is pioneering orbital biomanufacturing using microgravity to produce novel pharmaceuticals. Their first mission, W-1, launched in 2023. Key backers include Founders Fund, Lux Capital, and General Catalyst.

Core value: access to conditions unreplicable on Earth for certain chemical crystallizations. Major risk: regulatory and reentry logistics.

2024 plans include a second launch and partnership with a major pharma.
